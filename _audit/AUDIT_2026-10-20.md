## A. Output quality (cheap, large, visible)
1. Shorts encoder: `compositor.py:71-78` VideoToolbox `-q:v 65`, keyframe every 0.4 s, no B-frames, 2.7–3.1 Mbps → libx264 `-crf 17 -preset slow -g 60 -bf 3 -profile:v high`. (P1, S)
2. Colour: BT.601 matrix, untagged (`compositor.py:115-126`) → BT.709 matrix + tags, lanczos/accurate_rnd. Brand blue/yellow drift on players today. (P1, S)
3. Loudness: Shorts −15.5 LUFS / −0.4 dBTP, 4.5–5.7 LU spread between clips (single words −11, sentences −17); lessons −18 LUFS. No loudnorm anywhere in src. → per-clip level match (exists in `gen_audio.py:290 level_match`) + final two-pass loudnorm −14 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP; outros re-rendered to the same target. (P1, S–M)
4. Lessons encoder: Manim default crf 23 → 275–480 kbps total; untagged → final ffmpeg pass `-crf 14 -preset slow -tune animation`, BT.709, faststart. (P1, S)
5. Darkening band computed against the OLD yellow headline (`text_contrast.py:47`), up to 52% over footage around an opaque page; costs 25 s per render → strength 0 for opaque-page types; blur + warm grade + vignette instead. (P1, S)
6. Final duration not gated after the outro (82.76 s passed with an 80 s max); `+faststart` missing. (P3, S)
